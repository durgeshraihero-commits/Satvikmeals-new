Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

This privacy policy describes how SatvikMeals collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Name and email address (from Google login)
  • Phone number (for delivery purposes)
  • Delivery address
  • Subscription preferences and order history

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process your subscriptions and payments
  • Deliver meals to your address
  • Send you important updates about your orders
  • Improve our services

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share necessary information with:

  • Payment processors (Instamojo) for payment processing
  • Delivery partners for meal delivery

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can request access to your personal data or ask us to delete it by contacting us on WhatsApp.
