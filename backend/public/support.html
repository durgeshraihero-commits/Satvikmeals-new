Frequently Asked Questions

Can I pause my subscription?
Yes. Go to your dashboard and click Pause on any active subscription. Your end date will be extended automatically.
What is the delivery time?
Lunch is delivered between 12–1pm and dinner between 7–8pm. Times may vary slightly by area.
Is the food really without onion and garlic?
Yes. Our Satvik meals are strictly prepared without onion or garlic. We maintain a separate cooking area for Satvik orders.
How do I get a refund?
See our Refund Policy. Contact us on WhatsApp and we will process your request within 5–7 business days.
