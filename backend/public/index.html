About Us

A meal service built on discipline and trust

SatvikMeals is a Patna-based 100% pure vegetarian tiffin service committed to providing fresh, hygienic, and home-style meals to students, professionals, and families across the city.

Every meal is prepared with the same care and attention you would expect in your own home. We do not compromise on hygiene, ingredient quality, or the consistency of taste.

Our Philosophy

What Satvik means to us

01
The Satvik Standard
